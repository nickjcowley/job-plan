Base hospital

Your base hospital is the site you are contracted to and normally travel to. One PA is 4 hours. Each slot in the planner is a full-day list of 10 hours, which is 2.50 PA when it is at your base hospital. A full-day list at any other site attracts an extra hour of travel time (30 min each way), making it 11 hours or 2.75 PA. Choosing the base hospital therefore changes the PA value of every planner session at the other two sites.

Running total

Copy link saves your current configuration into the page address: every stepper value, toggle, the base hospital, the cycle length and each session you have marked in the planner (including any theatre or list text). Click it, then paste the link into an email, a note or a message. Anyone opening that link sees the calculator exactly as you left it, and you can bookmark it to come back to a scenario later. Nothing is stored on a server — the link itself carries the settings — so make a fresh link after any change. Reset to defaults clears everything back to the starting values.
