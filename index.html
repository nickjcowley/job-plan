The weekend rota PA calculation is unaffected by the leave system. The contract specifies 10 sessions per year (5 weekends); the 42-week attendance figure divides the resulting annual PAs to give the weekly average — consistent with all timetabled anaesthetic sessions.
Leave system difference — note for session placement. The ICU rota used annualised leave; anaesthetics uses standard NHS leave taken in blocked weeks (encouraged, not mandated). A week with only one session uses a full week of annual leave entitlement to avoid that session — proportionally poor value compared with a week carrying three or four sessions. Loading the additional sessions into weeks 1 and 6 (currently the two sparse weeks) makes them denser and therefore more worthwhile to take as leave.

Optimal PA Target and Reasoning

Rounding bands (nearest 0.25 PA)

Below 9.875 → 9.75 Rounds down — shortfall of 0.25 PA
9.875 – 10.000 → 10.00 ✓ Target zone: rounds to 10.00, no unpaid work
10.001 – 10.124 → 10.00 ✗ Rounds to 10.00 but working above contract without pay
10.125 – 10.374 → 10.25 Rounds up — above 10 PA contract target
Key constraint. The plan must not produce a calculated total between 10.001 and 10.124 PAs. In that band the work exceeds the 10 PA contracted rate but rounds down to 10.00, resulting in unpaid clinical sessions.
Transition period — ICU rota continues for 6–12 months. The ITU governance and informatics roles will run for at least another year, and the ICU annualised rota will remain in place during that time. This means the job plan during the transition period will carry both the residual ICU annualised sessions and the new anaesthetics timetable simultaneously. The total PA during transition will therefore differ from the steady-state plan below, and a formally agreed phased arrangement should be documented at the job planning meeting to avoid ambiguity about expectations during the overlap.
